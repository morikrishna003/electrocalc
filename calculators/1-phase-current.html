1-Phase AC Current Calculator

*Usually between 0.8 and 1 for residential/commercial appliances.

📖 Formula

If Power is in Watts (W):
I = P ÷ (V × pf)

If Power is in Kilowatts (kW):
I = (P × 1000) ÷ (V × pf)

I = Current in Amps (A)
P = Power
V = Voltage in Volts (V)
pf = Power Factor
